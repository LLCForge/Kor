# Code of conduct

Kor follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and never harass,
insult or publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a GitHub Security Advisory marked
"conduct", or by email to any maintainer of the repository. Reports are handled
confidentially; the reporter is told the outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent
ban, depending on the impact and whether the behaviour repeats.
